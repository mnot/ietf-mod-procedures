# IETF Moderation Procedures

This directory contains the documents produced by the IETF Moderator Team.

- [procedures.md](procedures.md): the moderation procedures that forum administrators and the
  moderator team follow when handling disruptive participation in IETF forums
- [scope.md](scope.md): which forums the procedures apply to, kept up to date by the moderator team
- [principles.md](principles.md): principles for making decisions under the procedures
- [newcomer-rejection-message.txt](newcomer-rejection-message.txt): the standard reply sent when a
  message from someone not subscribed to a list is not delivered

## Our mandate

[RFC9945](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9945.html) (IETF Community Moderation) sets out how the
IETF moderates disruptive participation across its public online forums. It creates a moderator
team, appointed by the IESG, which is responsible for establishing procedures to address moderation
needs across all IETF forums.

Section 4 of RFC9945 gives the moderator team its first task: to develop the procedures and
criteria for moderation, including the team's own operating procedures. These procedures must be
developed with community input, approved by the IESG before they take effect, and made public. They
need not be published as an RFC. These documents are the result of that work.

Until the IESG approves these procedures, the previous processes referenced in Section 1 of RFC9945
remain in effect.

## Who we are

The IESG appoints and recalls moderators (RFC9945, Section 2.1). Members of the IESG, the IAB, and
other NomCom-appointed bodies, as well as IETF LLC staff and contractors, may not serve on the team.

## Contact

The moderator team can be contacted at ietf-moderators@ietf.org, to report disruption, to ask for an
incident to be rescinded or a sanction reduced, or with any question about these procedures.

Community discussion of moderation policies and practices takes place on
[mod-discuss@ietf.org](https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/browse/mod-discuss/), which the IESG has
designated as the forum to "facilitate discussion about moderation" required by RFC9945.
